
# Company Overview

## 1. Organisation Details

**Organisation:** Bruges Retail Solutions (Fictional)  
**Industry:** E-commerce / Retail  
**Location:** Bruges, Belgium  
**Employees:** 40  

### Business Description

Bruges Retail Solutions is a small Belgian e-commerce company that sells consumer electronics and accessories to customers across Belgium and neighbouring EU countries.

The company operates primarily through its online store, with a small office and warehouse in Bruges. Its daily operations depend on the availability of its e-commerce platform, internal IT systems and third-party service providers.

## 2. IT Environment

The company relies on the following systems and services:

- **E-commerce Platform:** A cloud-hosted website used for online sales, customer accounts and order processing.
- **Microsoft 365:** Used for employee email, communication, document sharing and collaboration.
- **Cloud Storage:** Used to store business documents, financial reports and operational information.
- **Employee Devices:** Company laptops and desktop computers running Windows.
- **Customer Database:** Stores customer information, including names, email addresses, delivery addresses and order histories.
- **Payment Service Provider:** A third-party payment processor handles online transactions.
- **Warehouse Management System:** Used to track stock levels, process orders and manage deliveries.

## 3. Information Assets

The organisation processes and stores several types of information that require protection.

| Information Asset | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer Data | Names, contact information, delivery addresses and order histories |
| Employee Data | Personal information, employment records and payroll information |
| Financial Records | Invoices, financial reports and transaction records |
| Business Documents | Supplier contracts, internal procedures and business plans |
| User Credentials | Employee and administrator authentication information |
| Inventory Data | Product availability, stock records and warehouse information |

## 4. Business Priorities

The company's main priorities are:

1. **Service Availability:** Maintain the availability of the e-commerce platform to prevent interruptions to sales.
2. **Data Protection:** Protect customer and employee personal data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure.
3. **Operational Continuity:** Ensure that order processing, inventory management and deliveries can continue without significant disruption.
4. **Regulatory Compliance:** Meet applicable data protection and cybersecurity obligations, particularly GDPR requirements.
5. **Customer Trust:** Maintain customer confidence by protecting information and providing reliable services.

## 5. Assessment Objectives

The objective of this cybersecurity risk assessment is to:

- Identify cybersecurity risks affecting the organisation's information assets, systems and business operations.
- Evaluate the likelihood and potential business impact of identified risks.
- Assess existing security measures and identify areas requiring improvement.
- Recommend appropriate risk treatment strategies and security controls.
- Prioritise risks to support informed management decisions.

## 6. Assessment Scope

The assessment covers the following systems and processes:

- E-commerce platform and customer database.
- Microsoft 365 and employee account security.
- Cloud storage and business information.
- Employee endpoints and access management.
- Warehouse management and order processing systems.
- Cybersecurity dependencies involving third-party service providers.

The assessment focuses on cybersecurity risks affecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of business information and services.

Physical security, financial fraud unrelated to cybersecurity and detailed technical penetration testing are outside the scope of this assessment.

## 7. Assessment Assumptions

As Bruges Retail Solutions is a fictional organisation, the following assumptions apply:

- The company has a small internal IT team responsible for day-to-day IT operations.
- Some cybersecurity controls are implemented, but the organisation has not undergone a formal cybersecurity risk assessment.
- The organisation depends on third-party cloud and payment service providers.
- Specific technical vulnerabilities and the effectiveness of existing controls have not yet been independently verified.
- Risk assessments and recommendations will be based on the fictional business environment and clearly stated assumptions.

All findings and recommendations are for educational purposes and do not represent an assessment of a real organisation.
