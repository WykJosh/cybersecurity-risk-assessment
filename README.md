
# Cybersecurity Risk Assessment — Bruges Retail Solutions

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a simulated cybersecurity risk assessment for Bruges Retail Solutions, a fictional Belgian e-commerce company with 40 employees.

The assessment focuses on identifying, evaluating and treating cybersecurity risks that could affect the organisation's information assets, IT infrastructure and business operations.

The project was developed as part of my practical learning in Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC).

## Objectives

- Identify cybersecurity risks affecting business operations.
- Evaluate risks using a likelihood and impact scoring methodology.
- Prioritise risks based on their potential business impact.
- Recommend appropriate risk treatments and security controls.
- Estimate residual risks following proposed security measures.
- Communicate assessment findings through a management report.

## Methodology

Eight cybersecurity risks were identified and evaluated using a qualitative 5×5 risk matrix.

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

Risk classifications:

- Low: 1–5
- Medium: 6–14
- High: 15–25

Risk treatments were proposed based on the nature of each identified risk and its potential impact on the organisation.

Residual risk scores were estimated assuming the recommended controls were implemented effectively.

## Key Findings

The assessment identified:

- 5 High risks
- 3 Medium risks
- 0 Low risks

The highest-priority risk areas included phishing, ransomware, customer data breaches, e-commerce service disruption and unpatched systems.

Recommended treatments included multifactor authentication, endpoint protection, access controls, vulnerability management and business continuity measures.
