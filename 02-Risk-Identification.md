# Risk Identification

## **R-001** - Phishing and Account Compromise

**Asset:** Microsoft 365 employee accounts.

**Risk Scenario:** An employee may fall victim to a phishing attack and disclose their login credentials. If additional authentication controls are absent or bypassed, an attacker could gain unauthorised access to company emails and documents.

**Business Impact:** Exposure of sensitive business information, potential personal data breaches, disruption to internal communications and reputational damage.

## **R-002** - Ransomware

**Asset:** Employee devices and business files

**Risk scenario:** An employee device could become infected with ransomware through a malicious email attachment, compromised software or unauthorised access. This could result in the encryption or theft of sensitive company data, preventing employees from accessing critical business information.

**Business Impact:** Ransomware could disrupt daily business operations, cause financial losses through downtime and recovery costs, and potentially result in permanent data loss. If personal data is exposed, the company could also face regulatory consequences and reputational damage.

## **R-003** - Customer Data Breach

**Asset:** Customer database

**Risk scenario:** A malicious actor could gain unauthorised access to the customer database through compromised administrator credentials or vulnerabilities in the e-commerce platform, potentially resulting in the theft or exposure of sensitive customer information.

**Business Impact:** Exposure of customer information could result in reputational damage, loss of customer trust, financial costs associated with breach investigation and potential regulatory consequences under GDPR.
 
## **R-004** - Cloud Storage Exposure

**Asset:** Business documents stored in cloud storage

**Risk scenario:** Misconfigured access permissions or compromised employee credentials could allow unauthorised individuals to access sensitive company documents stored in the cloud.

**Business Impact:** Exposure of confidential business information could result in financial losses, reputational damage and the disclosure of sensitive commercial information. If personal data is exposed, the organisation could also face regulatory consequences under GDPR.

## **R-005** - E-commerce Service Disruption

**Asset:** E-commerce platform and online sales operations

**Risk scenario:** A Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attack or exploitation of vulnerabilities in the e-commerce platform could make the company's website unavailable, preventing customers from accessing the online store or completing purchases.

**Business Impact:** Extended website downtime could result in lost revenue, interrupted order processing and increased recovery costs. Prolonged disruption could also damage customer trust and negatively affect the company's reputation.

## **R-006** - Unpatched Systems

**Asset:** Company IT infrastructure, including employee devices and servers

**Risk scenario:** Failure to apply security patches and software updates in a timely manner could leave company systems vulnerable to known security flaws. Attackers could exploit these vulnerabilities to gain unauthorised access, deploy malware or compromise business systems.

**Business Impact:** Exploitation of unpatched systems could result in operational disruption, financial losses, data breaches and increased incident recovery costs. Depending on the systems affected, the organisation could also experience reputational damage and regulatory consequences.

## **R-007** - Third-party Security Incident

**Asset:** Third-party service providers and dependent business operations

**Risk scenario:** A cybersecurity incident affecting a third-party service provider, such as the company's payment processor or cloud hosting provider, could disrupt services or expose information shared with that provider.

**Business Impact:** A third-party incident could prevent customers from completing purchases, interrupt business operations and cause financial losses. If customer information is compromised, the organisation could also experience reputational damage and potential regulatory consequences.
 
## **R-008** - Unauthorised Access

**Asset:** Employee and administrator accounts, internal business systems

**Risk scenario:** Inadequate access controls, excessive user permissions or failure to revoke access when employees leave the organisation could allow unauthorised individuals to access sensitive systems and business information.

**Business Impact:** Unauthorised access could result in the theft, modification or deletion of sensitive information, disruption to business operations and financial losses. Exposure of personal data could also lead to regulatory consequences under GDPR and damage the organisation's reputation.
